Philosopher Spotlight

Marcus Aurelius

121 – 180 AD · Roman Emperor & Stoic

  • Last of Rome's "Five Good Emperors," ruling nearly two decades
  • Wrote Meditations as a private journal — never meant to be published
  • Governed an empire while enduring plagues, wars, and personal loss
  • Believed virtue alone was the only true good — not wealth or power
  • His core teaching: control what you can; accept what you cannot

Philosopher Spotlight

Friedrich Nietzsche

1844 – 1900 · German Philosopher

  • Declared "God is dead" — not as celebration, but as a diagnosis of modern culture
  • Introduced Übermensch: the individual who creates their own values
  • Suffered a mental breakdown in 1889 and never recovered
  • Close friends with Richard Wagner before a dramatic public falling out
  • His answer to suffering: passionate creative will — not escape
